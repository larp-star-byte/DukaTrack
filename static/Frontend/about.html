About DukaTrack

Small dukas in Kenya often run out of fast-selling goods while slow ones sit on the shelf. Owners usually decide what to buy from memory, so money is lost on both sides.

DukaTrack replaces the guessing. It keeps a record of every sale and every delivery, learns the shop's weekly selling pattern and predicts the demand for the coming days.

Who uses it

  • Shop Owner: manages products, stock-in, forecasts, reports, attendant accounts and backups.
  • Shop Attendant: logs in, records sales, and views stock levels and forecasts (read-only).

How the forecast works

With four weeks or more of sales, DukaTrack uses the weekly pattern (for example busy Saturdays). With less history it uses a simpler trend, and with very little it uses the average. Each forecast is saved and later compared with what was actually sold, so the owner can see how far to trust it.

Notices

  • Built as an Information Systems project.
  • The forecast shows predicted demand and whether the stock now is enough.
