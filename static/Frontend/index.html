Know what to stock before it runs out

DukaTrack helps the owner of a small Kenyan duka record stock and sales, and forecasts how much of each product will be sold in the next 7 or 30 days.

The forecast is made from the shop's own sales history and is shown next to the stock now, so the owner can see what to buy before the shelf is empty.

Log in to the shop

What DukaTrack does

  • Record

    Stock-in when goods arrive and a sale at the counter. Stock updates at once.

  • Forecast

    Predicted demand for each product for the next 7 or 30 days.

  • Report

    Sales by day, week or month, a CSV file, and how accurate past forecasts were.

Notices

  • Only registered people can log in.
  • The Shop Owner gives the Shop Attendant an account.
